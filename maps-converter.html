Google Maps → Apple Maps

Paste a Google Maps link and get the same place or route in Apple Maps.

Open in Apple Maps
Which links work?
  • Place links: google.com/maps/place/Name/@22.28,114.15,17z
  • Search links: google.com/maps/search/coffee or ?q=...
  • Directions: google.com/maps/dir/A/B or ?saddr=...&daddr=...
  • Walking directions is on by default: the link opens a walking route from where you are. Turn it off to just show the place.
  • If Apple Maps doesn’t find the place, tap Copy address and paste it into Apple Maps’ search box.
  • Plain coordinates like 22.2819, 114.1580 or a typed address.

Short links (maps.app.goo.gl/…) hide the location, and browsers block this page from opening them. Open the short link first, then copy the full address from the browser bar and paste it here.

Links with ftid= or cid= (from “Copy link” in the Google Maps iPhone app) only hold a Google place ID. Paste the place name or address instead, or open the place in Google Maps in Safari and copy the longer link.
